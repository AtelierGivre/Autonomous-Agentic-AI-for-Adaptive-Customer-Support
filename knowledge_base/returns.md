# Returns and refunds

Unused items in their original packaging can be returned within 30 calendar days
of delivery. Opened consumables, hygiene products and personalised items cannot
be returned unless they arrived damaged or faulty.

## How to start a return

1. Open the order in your account and choose "Return or replace".
2. Pick a reason and print the prepaid label.
3. Drop the parcel at any accepted carrier point.

Returns are inspected within 3 business days of arrival. Once approved, refunds
are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days. Bank posting
times can add 1-3 business days depending on the issuer.

## Damaged or wrong item

Report a damaged or incorrect item within 48 hours of delivery and include a
photo. We cover return shipping for damaged, defective or incorrectly shipped
items. Do not send a damaged electrical item back before support confirms the
return, for safety reasons.

## Non-refundable charges

Original express shipping fees are refunded only when the whole order is
returned because it was damaged, defective or incorrect. Cash-on-delivery
handling fees are non-refundable.
