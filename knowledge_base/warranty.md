# Hardware warranty

Networking hardware carries a 12-month limited warranty from the delivery date.
The warranty covers manufacturing defects and unexpected hardware failure. It
does not cover physical damage, liquid damage, unauthorised modification,
lightning damage or normal wear.

## Claim process

1. Troubleshoot first using the router guide; many faults are configuration, not
   hardware.
2. If the fault persists, support opens an RMA ticket and issues a return label.
3. Once the unit is received, a replacement ships within 3 business days.

Keep the serial number and proof of purchase. A replacement carries the
remainder of the original warranty, not a new 12-month term.

## Out-of-warranty repair

Out-of-warranty units can be repaired for a quoted fee. The quote is sent before
any work starts, and no repair happens without your approval.
