# Billing and payments

We accept major credit and debit cards, UPI and cash on delivery where available.
The charge appears on your statement under the store name shown at checkout.

## Duplicate or unexpected charge

A pending authorisation can look like a duplicate charge. Authorisations are
released by the bank within 3-5 business days and are not captured charges. If
two captured charges exist for one order, send the order number and the last four
digits of the card; support refunds the duplicate within 5-7 business days.

## Invoices

A GST invoice is emailed after dispatch. It can also be downloaded from the order
page. To change the billing name or GST number, request the change before the
invoice is generated.

## Refund timing

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. Card refunds take 5-7
business days after approval, UPI usually 2-3 business days, and cash-on-delivery
refunds are sent by bank transfer after the return is received.

## Failed payments

A failed payment usually means the bank declined the transaction or the billing
address does not match. Try another method; the order is not created until a
payment succeeds.
