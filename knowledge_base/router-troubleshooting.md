# Router troubleshooting

Use this order. Stop as soon as the connection is stable.

1. **Power cycle.** Unplug the router, wait 30 seconds, plug it back in. Wait up
   to 3 minutes for the internet LED to turn solid white.
2. **Check the LEDs.** Solid white means the WAN link is up. Flashing amber means
   the router cannot see the modem. Solid red means no internet from the line.
3. **Check the cable.** The modem cable must be in the WAN port, not a LAN port.
   Reseat both ends until they click.
4. **Separate the bands.** Devices more than 8 metres away or behind two walls
   should use the 2.4 GHz network. The 5 GHz band is faster but shorter range.
5. **Reduce interference.** Keep the router off the floor, away from microwaves,
   cordless phone bases and metal cabinets.
6. **Update firmware.** The app shows the installed version under Device >
   Software. Install any update, then wait for the reboot to finish.
7. **Factory reset.** Hold the reset pin for 10 seconds. This erases the Wi-Fi
   name, password and any custom settings, so reconfigure them afterwards.

## If it still disconnects

Capture the times of three disconnects and whether the internet LED changes.
Repeated drops with a red or amber LED point to the line or modem; drops with a
solid white LED point to Wi-Fi interference or too many devices. Support needs
those details to decide between a line escalation and a hardware replacement.
