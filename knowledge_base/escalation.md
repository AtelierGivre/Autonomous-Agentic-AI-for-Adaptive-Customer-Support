# Support hours and escalation

Chat support is available 9am to 9pm IST, Monday to Saturday. The assistant can
answer policy, order and troubleshooting questions outside those hours, but a
human reply waits until the next shift.

## Ticket response targets

| Priority | Example | First response |
|---|---|---|
| Normal | return question, invoice copy | within 24 business hours |
| High | delayed parcel, duplicate charge | within 8 business hours |
| Urgent | safety issue, account takeover | within 2 hours during support hours |

## When to escalate to a human

Escalate when the customer asks for a human, when the same troubleshooting step
has failed twice, when a refund or replacement needs approval, or when the
request involves account ownership or a privacy concern.

A ticket needs the customer's clear confirmation before it is created. The
assistant proposes the ticket, the customer replies YES, and only then is the
ticket opened. The confirmation expires after 10 minutes.

## What a human needs

Order number, the email on the account, a short description, and any error
message or photo. Never ask for a password, full card number or one-time code.
