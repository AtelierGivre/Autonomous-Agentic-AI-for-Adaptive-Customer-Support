# Shipping and delivery

Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days after dispatch. Express delivery takes
1-2 business days. Orders placed after 4pm are dispatched the next business day.

Every order gets a tracking link by email once the carrier scans the parcel. We
ship with BlueDart and Delhivery. The tracking page is the fastest way to see a
live location.

## Delayed orders

A parcel is considered delayed when it has not moved for 48 hours past its
estimated delivery date. Weather, regional hub congestion and incorrect pin
codes are the usual causes. If an order is delayed, the order record shows a
revised estimated delivery date and the reason where the carrier provides one.

If a delayed parcel has no update for 5 business days, support can open an
investigation ticket with the carrier. You do not need to contact the carrier
yourself.

## Missing or delivered-but-not-received

Check the delivery photo, the building reception and neighbours first. If the
parcel is marked delivered but you cannot find it, contact support within 48
hours so we can open a carrier investigation. Do not share another person's
order number; we can only discuss orders on your own account.

## Address changes

An address can be changed only before dispatch. Once the carrier has the parcel,
the address cannot be edited, and the parcel may be returned to the sender.
