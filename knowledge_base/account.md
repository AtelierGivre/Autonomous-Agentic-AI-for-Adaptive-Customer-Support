# Account and security

## Password reset

Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page. The reset link is valid for 30
minutes and can be used once. If the email does not arrive, check spam and
confirm the address on the account; support cannot read or set a password.

## Change email or phone

Sign in, open Account > Personal details, and confirm the change with the
one-time code sent to the current email or phone. If you no longer have access
to the old address, support must verify your identity before any change.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication can be enabled under Account > Security. Recovery
codes are shown once; store them offline. Support cannot bypass two-factor
authentication, and will never ask for a one-time code.

## Suspicious activity

If you see an order you did not place, change your password immediately and
contact support. Do not share your password, full card number or one-time
codes. Support only needs the order number and the last four digits of a card.
